# Amammre Wears — product image briefs

Review proposals only. No products or variants have been added to the database. Descriptions, material, construction, sizes, and colours are proposed specifications; confirm them against the actual product before publication. Images must depict the specified item and variant, not a different garment. Prices, stock, origin and craftsmanship claims require separate confirmation.

## Shared image direction

- Square 1:1 ecommerce images. Show the complete product clearly, centered, with enough space around it. Use a clean warm off-white background and natural, even light for main images.
- Produce a main front view, back view, and a close-up of the print or construction for each variant. Add an on-model image where appropriate. Keep the same item, print, colour placement, proportions and construction across all views.
- Generate one reference image per colourway; size variants of the same colourway can share images. Show the size in product data, not as text printed on the image.
- Avoid text, logos, watermarks, extra items, and claims about textile origin or handwork unless independently confirmed. Use natural, realistic Ghanaian models for on-model shots. Children's images should show fully clothed age-appropriate outfits.
- Image naming: `product-slug_colour_front.png`, `_back.png`, `_detail.png`, and `_model.png`. For example `northern-heritage-smock_navy-white_front.png`.
- Differentiate handwoven kente from kente-inspired printed fabric. Do not represent printed fabric as handwoven cloth.
